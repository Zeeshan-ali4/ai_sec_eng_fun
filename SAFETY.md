# Repository Safety Standard

This repository is a safe, lawful, and respectful collaboration space.

## Mandatory Boundaries

- Work must comply with applicable laws, hackathon rules, licenses, and organizational policies.
- Do not create, request, store, or distribute malware, destructive code, credential theft tools, unauthorized access tooling, exploit payloads intended for misuse, or instructions that facilitate wrongdoing.
- Do not create or distribute abusive, hateful, harassing, sexually exploitative, graphic, or otherwise harmful content, including offensive generated images.
- Do not target real people, organizations, accounts, devices, or services without explicit authorization.
- Do not use real secrets, credentials, private personal data, or confidential datasets. Use synthetic, anonymized, or properly licensed data.
- Security testing must be defensive, authorized, isolated, and limited to systems intentionally provided for testing.
- Stop work that creates a credible risk of harm. Document the concern and redesign the approach before continuing.

## Safe Development Practices

- Prefer simulations, mock systems, sandboxes, and non-deployable demonstrations.
- Apply least privilege and keep secrets outside version control.
- Validate inputs and outputs, and add safeguards against misuse.
- Review datasets and generated outputs for privacy, bias, harmful content, and licensing concerns.
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly; do not publish actionable details before remediation or approval.

## Reporting Concerns

Raise safety concerns with the repository maintainers or hackathon organizers. Content that violates this standard should be removed and reviewed before work resumes.
